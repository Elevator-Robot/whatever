# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - Cat Video EDC System

## Get Started in 30 Seconds!

1. **Open the App**: Simply open `index.html` in your web browser
2. **Add Your First Cat Video**: 
   - Click "📝 Capture Data" tab
   - Enter a video title (only required field)
   - Add any additional details you want
   - Click "💾 Save Video Data"
3. **View Your Collection**: Click "📋 View Records" to see all saved videos
4. **Try the Demo Data**: Import `demo-data.json` from the "💾 Export/Import" tab

## 🎯 Pro Tips

- **Auto-Save**: Your form data is automatically saved as you type
- **Search**: Use the search box to quickly find videos by title, notes, or tags
- **Categories**: Filter your collection by category for easy browsing
- **Export**: Always backup your data by exporting to JSON files
- **Mobile**: The system works great on phones and tablets too!

## 📝 Example Entry

Title: "Funny Cat Reaction to Cucumber"

Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)
